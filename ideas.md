# AI Automation Roadmap — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### 1. Automation Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial learning field guide that makes automation feel practical, deliberate, and calm. It translates the research-and-roadmap character of The Data Tea into a new technical learning product.

**Probability:** 0.07

### 2. Control Room Atlas
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast systems map built from dense panels, status lights, and command-line cues. It would portray AI automation as a live operations discipline.

**Probability:** 0.04

### 3. Workshop Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, tactile maker’s workbook using structured lessons, handwritten-style annotations, and material texture. It would make an intimidating topic feel accessible and human.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach: Automation Field Notes

### Design Movement
**Contemporary editorial systems design**, informed by technical field guides, scientific notebooks, and high-quality magazine layouts. The experience should feel like a focused learning instrument, rather than a generic course marketplace.

### Core Principles
1. **Make progression visible:** learning should feel like traversing a mapped route, not browsing a feed.
2. **Teach through artifacts:** each section surfaces a real build, tool, or workflow output as the unit of learning.
3. **Use measured contrast:** deep ink ground, warm paper surfaces, and decisive accent signals establish hierarchy without visual noise.
4. **Invite momentum:** concise next actions, small completion cues, and tactile cards reward movement through the path.

### Color Philosophy
The base is **deep blue-black ink** to create focus and align with The Data Tea’s calm technical tone. Warm **paper cream** softens reading-intensive areas and makes the site feel editorial. **Signal amber** is reserved for active guidance, milestones, and CTAs; **tidepool mint** denotes tools, integrations, and verified readiness. Color should communicate function, not decorate every surface.

### Layout Paradigm
A **vertical expedition map** replaces a conventional centered marketing page. The first viewport introduces the path alongside a live “route card”; the core learning sequence moves down an asymmetric left-hand timeline, alternating large content plates with compact tool stations. A sticky mini-route navigator supports long-form exploration on desktop.

### Signature Elements
1. **Route stamps:** outlined serial labels such as `01 / Orient` make each phase feel like a distinct stretch of the journey.
2. **Flow-line diagrams:** gentle dotted paths and branching connector strokes visually tie modules, tools, and outcomes together.
3. **Field note cards:** warm, subtly textured paper panels that contain practical prompts, deliverables, and resources.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should provide quiet confirmation. Roadmap nodes expand on demand, filter controls immediately reframe the curriculum, and completion toggles update a visible progress meter. Buttons feel firm and tactile; nothing should compete with the learner’s decision-making.

### Animation
Use short, structural motion only. On load, the route line and phase plates enter with staggered opacity-and-translate transitions (40–70ms between related elements). Hover states lift cards by 2–4px and reveal a signal-colored rule. Expanding a module should use a restrained 180–240ms opacity/transform transition. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no looping decorative animation.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** provides a confident, editorial voice for page titles and milestone headings. **Manrope** serves the body and UI at medium-to-high legibility. **IBM Plex Mono** is used selectively for route labels, tools, metadata, and workflow syntax. Headlines are compact and slightly condensed through tracking; body text stays generous and readable.

### Brand Essence
**The Data Tea AI Automation Path turns curious professionals into capable workflow builders through a project-led, tool-aware learning route.**

Personality adjectives: **grounded, exacting, encouraging**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and outcome-led; CTAs use active verbs and microcopy reads like a helpful instructor who respects the learner’s time. Avoid hype, abstraction, and generic onboarding language.

Example lines:
- “Build the workflow you wish your team already had.”
- “Start with one trigger. End with a useful system.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a **tea-leaf circuit mark**: three tapered leaf forms orbit a small node, with a single connected trace suggesting an automation flow. The standalone symbol appears in the header and favicon; the `THE DATA TEA` wordmark is set in a custom editorial small-caps lockup rather than a default font treatment.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Amber — `#E7B463`**. It marks the next actionable step, completed milestone, and key destination along the path.
